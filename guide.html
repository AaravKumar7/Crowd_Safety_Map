Safety Guide

Practical tips and procedures for your safety.

VERIFIED TIPS

Personal Safety

Street Safety

Walk in well-lit, busy areas. Avoid shortcuts through isolated alleys or parks.

Commute Safety

Share your ride details with a friend. Always check the driver's ID and vehicle plate.

Digital Safety

Live Location Tracking

Use modern apps to share your real-time location with trusted contacts whenever you're traveling late or in unfamiliar areas.

Digital SOS

Configure your phone's power button shortcut to send an SOS message with your location to emergency contacts instantly.

Emergency Procedures

If You Are Followed:

1. Cross the street and see if the person follows.

2. Head towards a busy area (a shop, restaurant, or petrol station).

3. Do not go home if you are being followed.

4. Call the police or press the SafeZone SOS button.
